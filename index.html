Budget basics
How the daily number works
We take your monthly income, subtract your fixed bills, subtract what you've already spent this month, then split what's left across the days remaining. Spend less than that today and tomorrow's number goes up a little; spend more and it comes down. Nothing is hidden — it's just arithmetic, redone every day. This is the whole idea behind an ADHD-friendly budget: one forgiving number instead of a strict plan that falls apart after one off day.
